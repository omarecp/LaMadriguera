A quieter kind of coffee shop

La Madriguera is designed around small rituals: a steady espresso before work, a pastry after class, a table where a conversation can unfold without feeling rushed.

The brand can grow from here with real photos, a fuller menu, online ordering, and events. This starter page gives the shop a clean first impression while leaving room for personality.
